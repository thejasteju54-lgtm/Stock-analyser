# Sector Frameworks

## Purpose

Provide sector-specific analytical frameworks.

Do not use one generic valuation model for every company.

---

# Banks

Analyse:

- loan growth
- deposit growth
- CASA
- NIM
- ROA
- ROE
- GNPA
- NNPA
- credit cost
- slippages
- provision coverage
- capital adequacy
- cost-to-income
- unsecured exposure

Primary valuation:

- P/B
- P/E

---

# NBFCs

Analyse:

- AUM growth
- borrowing cost
- NIM
- spreads
- ROA
- ROE
- credit cost
- GNPA
- NNPA
- collection efficiency
- leverage
- liquidity

---

# Commodities

Analyse:

- realized prices
- production
- cost curve
- cycle position
- capex
- leverage
- FCF
- inventory
- commodity-price sensitivity

Avoid valuing peak-cycle earnings as permanent earnings.

---

# Insurance

Analyse:

- premium growth
- APE
- VNB
- VNB margin
- persistency
- solvency

- investment book

---

# General Rule

Sector classification must happen before valuation.

If the company has multiple business segments, analyse each material segment separately.

Do not force the company into a sector framework that does not fit its business model.
