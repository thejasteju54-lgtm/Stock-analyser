# Research Methodology

## Objective

Produce evidence-driven, skeptical and reproducible Indian equity research.

## Research Sequence

1. Verify company identity.
2. Inventory sources.
3. Classify documents.
4. Extract evidence.
5. Normalize financial data.
6. Validate evidence.
7. Calculate financial metrics.
8. Analyse fundamentals.
9. Perform forensic analysis.
10. Analyse management.
11. Analyse industry.
12. Analyse valuation.
13. Analyse technical structure.
14. Research current developments.
15. Identify catalysts.
16. Identify risks.
17. Build scenarios.
18. Challenge the thesis.
19. Verify final claims.
20. Generate verdict.

## Evidence Hierarchy

Prefer primary sources.

Highest priority:

- audited financial statements
- annual reports
- official filings
- official investor presentations
- official transcripts

Secondary sources should support rather than replace primary evidence when primary evidence is available.

## Fact Classification

Every important statement should be classified as:

FACT
CALCULATION
INTERPRETATION
MANAGEMENT CLAIM
ASSUMPTION
FORECAST
OPINION
UNVERIFIED

## Two-Year Analysis

Where two years of data exist, compare:

- revenue
- growth
- EBITDA
- margins
- PAT
- CFO
- FCF
- debt
- ROE
- ROCE
- working capital

Look for direction, not merely absolute values.

## Management Analysis

Compare historical promises with outcomes.

Repeated missed guidance should affect credibility.

## Forensic Analysis

Look for:

- cash-flow divergence
- receivables deterioration
- inventory anomalies
- debt increases
- related-party issues
- exceptional items
- auditor concerns
- dilution
- promoter activity

## Valuation

Ask:

"What expectations are embedded in the current valuation?"

Then test whether those expectations are realistic.

## Technical Analysis

Technical analysis should complement, not replace, fundamental analysis.

## Current Research

Separate current developments from historical financial data.

## Bear Case

Every thesis must include a serious attempt to disprove itself.

## Final Verdict

The system must make an explicit:

BUY
HOLD
AVOID

decision when evidence is sufficient.

If evidence is insufficient, explain what prevents a reliable decision.

## Core Principle

The goal is not prediction certainty.

The goal is better risk-adjusted decision-making through evidence, verification and disciplined reasoning.
