---
description: Perform a hostile verification audit of an equity research report before it is presented as complete.
---

Attempt to find errors, unsupported claims, fabricated numbers, incorrect calculations, source conflicts and analytical weaknesses.

## Step 1 — Source Audit

For every material claim verify:

- source exists
- source supports claim
- company/entity is correct
- reporting period is correct
- units are correct
- consolidated/standalone basis is correct

## Step 2 — Numerical Audit

Verify:

- revenue growth
- margins
- CFO/PAT
- FCF
- ROE
- ROCE
- debt ratios
- valuation calculations
- scenario calculations

Use deterministic calculations.

## Step 3 — Provenance Audit

Check that material facts contain:

- source
- page/section where applicable
- period
- confidence

## Step 4 — Contradiction Audit

Search for conflicting:

- financial values
- management statements
- shareholding values
- guidance
- market information

Do not silently resolve conflicts.

## Step 5 — Hallucination Audit

Search specifically for:

- invented numbers
- invented news
- invented management quotes
- unsupported peer comparisons
- unsupported technical levels
- unsupported causal claims
- false precision

## Step 6 — Management Audit

Check whether management claims were accidentally represented as facts.

## Step 7 — Current Information Audit

For current news verify:

- publication date
- source
- relevance
- whether information is actually current

## Step 8 — Valuation Audit

Verify:

- metric selection
- current values
- peer values
- historical values
- sector suitability

## Step 9 — Technical Audit

Verify that:

- support is actually visible/data-derived
- resistance is actually visible/data-derived
- trend claims are supported
- volume claims are supported

## Step 10 — Thesis Challenge

Ask:

"What evidence would make this verdict wrong?"

Search for that evidence.

## Step 11 — Final Classification

Classify findings:

PASS
WARNING
FAIL
CONFLICTING

## Output

Return:

Research verification status
Critical errors
Warnings
Unsupported claims
Source conflicts
Calculation errors
Missing evidence
Recommended corrections

Do not silently modify the research during verification.

Identify the issue first, then correct it only after establishing the cause.

## Completion Rule

Research is verified only when no critical unsupported claim or calculation error remains.
