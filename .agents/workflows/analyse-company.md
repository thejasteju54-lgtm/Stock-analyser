---
description: Run the complete evidence-driven equity research workflow for an Indian listed company.
---

Analyse the company as a senior Indian equity research analyst with approximately 20 years of experience across:

- fundamental analysis
- forensic accounting
- valuation
- management analysis
- technical analysis
- behavioural finance
- industry analysis

The objective is not to produce a generic AI summary.

The objective is to produce an evidence-backed, challengeable investment thesis.

## Step 0 — Company Verification

Confirm:

- company name
- ticker
- exchange
- sector
- entity identity

Do not analyse the wrong company.

## Step 1 — Evidence Inventory

Inventory:

- annual reports
- financial statements
- MDA
- concall transcripts
- investor presentations
- shareholding documents
- Screener screenshots
- technical charts
- current research
- other uploaded documents

Identify:

- reporting periods
- missing information
- duplicate documents
- conflicting sources
- source quality

## Step 2 — Document Processing

Use the document-ingestion skill.

Classify and extract documents.

Preserve:

- page
- section
- table
- period
- source
- confidence

## Step 3 — Financial Extraction

Use the financial-extraction skill.

Extract where available:

- revenue
- EBITDA
- EBIT
- PAT
- EPS
- CFO
- FCF
- debt
- cash
- net debt
- receivables
- inventory
- working capital
- ROE
- ROCE
- margins
- shareholding

## Step 4 — Financial Reconstruction

Use deterministic calculations.

Calculate:

- revenue growth
- EBITDA margin
- PAT margin
- CFO/PAT
- FCF
- ROE
- ROCE
- debt ratios
- interest coverage
- working-capital trends

Never ask the LLM to perform important arithmetic manually.

## Step 5 — Fundamental Analysis

Determine:

- business quality
- revenue quality
- earnings quality
- cash generation
- balance-sheet strength
- capital allocation
- return sustainability

Identify deterioration and improvement.

## Step 6 — Forensic Analysis

Use the forensic-analysis skill.

Investigate:

- receivables
- inventory
- working capital
- cash conversion
- related parties
- exceptional items
- contingent liabilities
- auditor observations
- debt
- dilution
- promoter activity

Rank red flags.

Never claim fraud without evidence.

## Step 7 — Management DNA

Use management-dna.

Analyse:

- guidance
- promises
- execution
- explanations
- confidence
- defensiveness
- changes in language

Track:

Promise → Outcome → Variance → Explanation

## Step 8 — Promoter & Ownership

Analyse:

- promoter holding
- promoter pledge
- stake changes
- institutional ownership
- material ownership changes

## Step 9 — Industry

Use industry-analysis.

Analyse:

- industry growth
- cyclicality
- competition
- regulation
- pricing power
- disruption
- structural trends

## Step 10 — Valuation

Use valuation-analysis.

First identify the business model and sector.

Then select appropriate valuation metrics.

Analyse:

- current valuation
- historical valuation where verified
- peer valuation where verified
- growth
- profitability
- cash generation

Never invent peer values.

## Step 11 — Technical Analysis

Use technical-analysis.

Analyse supplied chart and/or verified market data.

Determine:

- trend
- market structure
- accumulation
- markup
- distribution
- markdown
- support
- resistance
- volume
- momentum

Never invent technical levels.

## Step 12 — Current Research

Use news-intelligence.

Research verified current information:

- results
- company announcements
- contracts
- capex
- acquisitions
- regulatory changes
- management updates
- industry developments
- material news

Each item must contain:

- date
- source
- event
- relevance

## Step 13 — Catalysts

Identify and rank catalysts by:

- probability
- potential impact
- timeframe

## Step 14 — Risks

Identify the three risks most capable of destroying the thesis.

For each:

- risk
- probability
- impact
- early warning indicator
- consequence

## Step 15 — Bear Case

Actively attempt to disprove the thesis.

Search for:

- slowing growth
- margin deterioration
- debt problems
- weak cash flow
- management execution failures
- industry headwinds
- valuation compression

## Step 16 — Bull Case

Identify credible upside drivers.

Avoid unrealistic assumptions.

## Step 17 — Scenario Analysis

Create:

- Bear
- Base
- Bull

Every scenario must contain explicit assumptions.

## Step 18 — Behavioural Bias Check

Check for:

- confirmation bias
- narrative bias
- recency bias
- anchoring
- survivorship bias
- valuation optimism

## Step 19 — Evidence Verification

Use evidence-verification.

Before final verdict verify every material claim.

Remove or label unsupported claims.

## Step 20 — Final Verdict

Return:

### Verdict

BUY / HOLD / AVOID

### Conviction

X/10

### Short-Term Outlook

1 year

### Long-Term Outlook

5+ years

### Financial Quality

### Management Quality

### Valuation

### Technical Structure

### Major Catalysts

### Major Risks

### Thesis Breakers

### Interesting Valuation Condition

Do not fabricate a target price when sufficient data does not exist.

## Final Principle

Do not produce a diplomatic conclusion.

If the evidence says Avoid, say Avoid.

If the business is excellent but valuation is excessive, say so.

If evidence is insufficient, explicitly identify what is missing.

The verdict must be traceable to evidence.
