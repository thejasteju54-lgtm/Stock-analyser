# Evidence Verification Skill

Before a material claim enters the final report:

## Check 1 — Source

Does a source exist?

## Check 2 — Exactness

Does the source actually support the claim?

## Check 3 — Period

Is the period correct?

## Check 4 — Entity

Is the value for the correct company/entity?

## Check 5 — Accounting Basis

Standalone or consolidated?

## Check 6 — Unit

₹ crore, ₹ million, %, absolute value?

## Check 7 — Calculation

If derived, can the calculation be reproduced?

## Check 8 — Contradiction

Does another reliable source disagree?

## Check 9 — Freshness

For current information, is the source sufficiently recent?

## Check 10 — Interpretation

Is the statement fact or interpretation?

Label accordingly.

## Failure

If verification fails:

Do not include the claim as verified fact.

Mark it:

UNVERIFIED

or

CONFLICTING

or

DATA UNAVAILABLE
