# Forensic Analysis Skill

Act like a forensic equity analyst.

Investigate:

## Earnings Quality

Compare:

PAT
CFO
FCF

Investigate persistent divergence.

## Working Capital

Analyse:

receivables
inventory
payables
working-capital days

Look for unusual deterioration.

## Revenue Quality

Investigate:

rapid growth
customer concentration
receivable growth
contract changes
one-off revenue
channel stuffing indicators when evidence exists

Do not claim fraud without evidence.

## Balance Sheet

Investigate:

debt
net debt
interest
capitalisation
contingent liabilities
off-balance-sheet risks

## Accounting

Investigate:

exceptional items
capitalised expenses
impairment
depreciation
related parties
auditor qualifications
restatements

## Promoter

Investigate:

holding
pledge
buying
selling
dilution

Promoter stake reduction is not automatically proof of wrongdoing. Explain context and magnitude.

## Output

For each red flag:

Issue
Evidence
Why it matters
Severity
Probability
Potential impact
What would invalidate the concern

Never use sensational language without evidence.
