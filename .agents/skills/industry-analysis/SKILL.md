# Industry Analysis Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill when analyzing an industry, sector, competitive landscape, market structure, demand drivers, supply dynamics, regulation, or long-term business trends.

## Analysis Areas

Review:

- Industry size
- Growth rate
- Demand drivers
- Supply constraints
- Competitive intensity
- Key players
- Market share
- Pricing power
- Regulation
- Cyclicality
- Entry barriers
- Substitution risk
- Customer concentration
- Technology disruption
- Margin structure

## Rules

Do not invent industry size, growth rates, market share, or forecasts.

If verified data is unavailable, state:

"Industry data unavailable from verified sources."

## Output Format

Provide:

- Industry overview
- Key growth drivers
- Key risks
- Competitive landscape
- Position of the company
- Margin outlook
- Regulatory factors
- Long-term outlook
- Data gaps

## Evidence Requirement

Material industry claims must be supported by a source or clearly labeled as an assumption.
