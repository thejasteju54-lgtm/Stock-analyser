# News Intelligence Skill

## Purpose

Use this skill when tracking, summarizing, or interpreting company news, sector news, regulatory news, macro events, or market-moving developments.

## Source Priority

Prefer:

1. Official company filings
2. Exchange announcements
3. Regulator announcements
4. Company press releases
5. Reputable financial news sources
6. Earnings call transcripts
7. Industry publications

## Rules

Always distinguish between:

- Confirmed facts
- Management claims
- Analyst opinions
- Market rumors
- User-provided assumptions

Never present rumors as facts.

Always include the date of the news item.

## Output Format

For each news item, provide:

- Date
- Source
- Headline
- Summary
- Impact on company
- Impact on sector
- Positive, negative, or neutral classification
- Confidence level
- Follow-up questions

## Anti-Hallucination Requirement

If a source cannot be verified, write:

"Source not verified."
