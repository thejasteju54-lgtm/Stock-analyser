# Management DNA Skill

Analyse management like a skeptical institutional analyst.

## Extract

- promises
- targets
- guidance
- explanations
- risks
- confidence
- strategic claims
- capital allocation statements

## Promise Tracking

For every meaningful historical promise:

Promise
Date
Expected outcome
Actual outcome
Variance
Explanation

## Language Analysis

Compare periods for changes such as:

- increasing uncertainty
- stronger confidence
- defensive explanations
- repeated excuses
- changing terminology
- avoidance of direct answers
- aggressive guidance

Language change is a signal, not proof.

## Management Credibility

Assess:

execution
transparency
consistency
capital allocation
guidance accuracy
shareholder treatment

## Output

Produce:

Management strengths
Management weaknesses
Credibility assessment
Behavioural signals
Key unresolved questions

Never claim deception unless evidence supports it.
